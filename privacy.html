This Privacy Policy explains what information Teach Them To Love Outreach Ministries ("T3L," "we," "us," or "our"), 601 Quail Valley Drive, Georgetown, TX 78626, collects through the website at www.t3loutreach.org and our online portals (together, the "Site"), and how we use it. By using the Site, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information you give us

The public website has no sign-up or contact forms. You give us information when you:

  • Use the member portal. The portal's forms may ask for your name, email address, phone number, ministry role, and the text of a prayer request.
  • Call, text, or email us. We receive whatever you choose to share.
  • Donate or buy event tickets. These go through third-party services (PayPal, Cash App, Zeffy). They share with us details such as your name, email, and gift amount. We never receive your card or bank numbers.

Prayer requests and other personal details can be sensitive. Share only what you are comfortable sharing. If you are in danger, do not rely on the Site: call 911.

2. Information collected automatically

The public website does not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. Like any website, our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically records basic technical data when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, for security and reliability. The Site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers. The portals may save a small on/off setting in your browser's local storage.
