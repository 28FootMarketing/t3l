How we steward your trust

Accountability & transparency

We hold ourselves to the standard we ask of others — honesty, stewardship, and care for the people we serve.

Our mission

Teach Them To Love Outreach Ministries exists to teach people to love God and one another, and to meet real needs in our community through outreach, discipleship, and care.

Faithful stewardship

Gifts are directed to the ministry and programs they support. We keep overhead lean and review spending against our mission so that your generosity reaches the people it was meant for.

How we measure impact

We track participation, volunteer hours, gatherings, and lives touched through our member portal and volunteer records — the same figures shown above, drawn from live data rather than estimates.

Governance

Our ministry is led by a dedicated team and accountable leadership who guide programs, finances, and care with integrity.

Privacy & care

We protect the people who trust us — especially sensitive requests like prayer and personal need. See our Privacy Policy for how we handle personal information.

Registered 501(c)(3)

Teach Them To Love Outreach Ministries is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN 46-4850870) based in Georgetown, Texas. Gifts are tax-deductible as allowed by law. Grantmakers and partners can request our organizational details and financial summaries directly — we're glad to support your due diligence.
