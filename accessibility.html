Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed: November 2026

Teach Them To Love Outreach Ministries is committed to making our website and member portal usable by everyone, including people with disabilities. We believe access to our ministry, our resources, and our community should not depend on how a person reads, hears, or navigates the web.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, as our practical standard across the public website and the member portal. Accessibility is ongoing work, and we improve the Site over time.

What we do

  • Use clear headings, labels, and landmarks so screen readers can navigate the pages.
  • Provide a visible "skip to content" link and keyboard-reachable navigation and controls.
  • Aim for readable color contrast and text that resizes without breaking the layout.
  • Respect the "reduce motion" setting for animations.
  • Write status messages (such as form confirmations) so assistive technology can announce them.

Known limitations

Some third-party content and documents (for example, files linked from the resource library or external donation pages) may not yet be fully accessible. Where that happens, we will help you get the same information or complete the same task another way — just ask.

Need help or an accommodation?
